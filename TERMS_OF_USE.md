## 2. Description of the Service

Kapiert is an educational language-learning application designed to assist learners in mastering German noun genders (*der*, *die*, *das*), article rules, plurals, and vocabulary. Features include:
- Instant article and noun dictionary lookup powered by a curated database of 90,000+ nouns and open dictionary fallbacks.
- Interactive article quizzes with streak and mastery tracking.
- Word bookmarking (**Favorites**) for focused review and custom quiz sessions.
- Optional cloud account synchronization across devices via Supabase.
